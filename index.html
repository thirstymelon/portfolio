THE VIBE CODING PHILOSOPHY
I build as a vibe coder. I actively use AI tools—including ChatGPT, AntiGravity, and Claude—throughout every project building phase to accelerate creative brainstorming, explore design alternatives, and craft exhaustive documentation.
However, all architectural decisions, low-level register manipulations, memory layouts, and critical parts of the projects were conceived and implemented by me. AI is my sparring partner for brainstorming and documentation; the bare-metal engineering is 100% human-directed.
